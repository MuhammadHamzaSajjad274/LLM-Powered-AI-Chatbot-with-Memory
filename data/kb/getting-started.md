# Getting Started with FlowBoard

FlowBoard is a cloud-based project management platform designed for software, marketing, and operations teams who need visual workflows without enterprise complexity. This guide walks you through creating your workspace, inviting teammates, and completing your first project in under fifteen minutes.

## Creating Your Workspace

After signing up at app.flowboard.io, you'll choose a workspace name (typically your company or team name) and select your primary use case: software delivery, marketing campaigns, or general operations. FlowBoard creates a default **Home** board with three starter columns: Backlog, In Progress, and Done. You can rename these immediately or keep them while learning the interface.

Your workspace URL follows the pattern `flowboard.io/w/your-workspace`. Workspace owners can configure timezone, default notification preferences, and whether guests are allowed on shared boards.

## Inviting Your Team

Navigate to **Settings → Team** and enter colleague email addresses. FlowBoard sends invitations with one of four roles: **Viewer** (read-only), **Member** (create and edit tasks), **Admin** (manage boards and automations), or **Owner** (billing and workspace deletion). Invitations expire after seven days; admins can resend them from the pending invites list.

## Your First Board and Task

Open the Home board and click **Add Task** in the Backlog column. Enter a title, optional description, assignee, and due date. Drag the task card to **In Progress** when work begins. FlowBoard autosaves every change—there is no manual save button. Use `@mentions` in task comments to notify teammates.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Press `?` anywhere in FlowBoard to open the shortcut panel. Common shortcuts include `N` for new task, `/` for global search, and `G then B` to jump to boards. Mobile users get swipe gestures for moving cards between columns.

## Next Steps

Explore **Automations** to reduce manual status updates, connect **Slack** or **GitHub** under Integrations, and review **Billing & Plans** if you need more than three boards on the Free tier.
