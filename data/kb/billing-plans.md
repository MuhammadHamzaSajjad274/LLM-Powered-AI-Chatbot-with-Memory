# Billing and Plans

FlowBoard offers four subscription tiers: **Free**, **Pro**, **Business**, and **Enterprise**. Billing is per workspace, not per user, though seat limits apply. All paid plans include a 14-day trial without credit card required.

## Plan Comparison

**Free** — Up to 5 members, 3 active boards, 100 MB file storage, 3 automations per board, 7-day activity history. Suitable for individuals and small experiments.

**Pro** ($12/member/month, billed annually) — Unlimited boards, 5 GB storage per member, 25 automations per board, unlimited activity history, Timeline and Sprint views, priority email support (24-hour response).

**Business** ($24/member/month) — Everything in Pro plus SSO (SAML), audit logs, custom fields, advanced permissions, 99.9% uptime SLA, dedicated CSM for 50+ seats.

**Enterprise** (custom pricing) — VPC deployment option, HIPAA BAA, custom data retention, SCIM provisioning. Contact sales@flowboard.io.

## Payment and Invoices

Workspace Owners manage billing under **Settings → Billing**. FlowBoard accepts Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and ACH for annual Enterprise contracts. Invoices generate on the renewal date and are available as PDF in the billing portal. Failed payments retry three times over seven days before downgrade to Free.

## Upgrades, Downgrades, and Refunds

Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges. Downgrades apply at the next renewal cycle—paid features remain until then. Refunds are available within 30 days of initial purchase for annual plans only; contact support with invoice number.

## Seat Management

Removing a member frees a seat at the next billing cycle. Deactivated (not deleted) members retain read-only access without consuming a seat on Business and above. Guest viewers on public boards never count toward seat limits.

## Nonprofit and Education Discounts

Verified nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive 50% off Pro and Business. Apply at flowboard.io/pricing/nonprofit with documentation; approval takes 3–5 business days.
